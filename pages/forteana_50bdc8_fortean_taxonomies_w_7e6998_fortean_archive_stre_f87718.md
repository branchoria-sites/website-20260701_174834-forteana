## Introduction

The value of the *Fortean Times* archive lies not simply in preserving strange stories but in making them comparable. [Forteana]({{ 'forteana/' | relative_url }}) has always depended on collecting scattered reports from [newspapers]({{ 'newspapers/' | relative_url }}), letters, books and personal testimony. By organising those reports into recurring editorial streams rather than treating every anomaly as an isolated curiosity, *Fortean Times* created a practical way to recognise recurring motifs while preserving uncertainty. Its archive functions less like a catalogue of solved mysteries than a machine for generating comparisons: every new report is read alongside dozens of older ones, inviting readers to ask whether they are seeing repetition, coincidence, folklore, media influence or something genuinely unexplained. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortean_Times" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fortean Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fortean Times</span></span></span>

## Why recurring categories make archives usable

[Charles Fort]({{ 'charles-fort/' | relative_url }}) himself assembled thousands of press cuttings because he believed isolated anomalies gained meaning when viewed together rather than separately. *Fortean Times* extended that principle into a long-running editorial system. Instead of publishing unrelated oddities in random order, it developed recurring sections devoted to [ghosts]({{ 'ghosts/' | relative_url }}), UFOs, cryptozoology, archaeology, strange deaths, reader experiences, myths and historical curiosities. The result is not a rigid taxonomy but an archive where similar claims accumulate over decades. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://sacred-texts.com/fort/index.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sacred-texts.com">[Internet Sacred Text Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sacred-texts.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Internet Sacred Text Archivethe complete works of charles fortWorks of Charles Fort and texts on unexplained phenomena, anomalies, and pa...</span></span></span>

This continuity changes how readers encounter the material. A report of an unexplained light is no longer judged only against ordinary astronomy or aviation. It can also be compared with previous waves of similar reports, differences in witness testimony, geographical clusters and changing cultural interpretations. Likewise, an unusual animal sighting becomes part of a much larger stream of cryptozoological claims rather than remaining an isolated newspaper curiosity. The archive therefore encourages comparative reading rather than simple belief or disbelief. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortean_Times" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fortean Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fortean Times</span></span></span>

The magazine's long publication history reinforces this effect. Beginning as *The News* in 1973 before becoming *Fortean Times* in 1976, it has accumulated decades of recurring coverage, allowing readers to compare reports across generations instead of within a single news cycle. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortean_Times" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fortean Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fortean Times</span></span></span>

## What editorial streams reveal and conceal

Regular columns perform an important editorial function. They reveal patterns by ensuring that similar material is consistently collected, but they also influence which patterns become visible.

Several long-running features illustrate this process:

* **Strange Days** gathers unusual news stories from newspapers around the world, creating a continuous record of contemporary anomalies rather than isolated [headlines]({{ 'headlines/' | relative_url }}).
* **Ghostwatch**, **Alien Zoo** and **UFO Files** maintain specialised streams that allow readers to compare reports within particular traditions.
* **Strange Deaths** assembles unusual fatalities that would otherwise disappear into local reporting.
* **Mythconceptions** deliberately tests popular claims against available evidence, preventing the archive from becoming merely a repository of [folklore]({{ 'folklore/' | relative_url }}).
* **Fortean Bureau of Investigation** revisits older cases, showing how interpretations change as new evidence or better historical research emerges. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortean_Times" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fortean Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fortean Times</span></span></span>

Because these streams continue month after month, they produce a form of editorial memory. A single report rarely appears decisive, but repeated reporting allows readers to notice recurring witness descriptions, repeated investigative mistakes, persistent rumours and recurring hoaxes.

At the same time, editorial organisation inevitably shapes perception. A mystery assigned to a UFO column may be read differently from the same report discussed as folklore, psychology or atmospheric science. The archive therefore does not merely record patterns; it participates in creating the categories through which later readers recognise those patterns. This is one reason *Fortean Times* has consistently described its editorial stance as one of "benevolent scepticism" rather than advocacy for any single paranormal interpretation. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortean_Times" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fortean Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fortean Times</span></span></span>

## How comparison creates new questions

The archive's greatest strength is that comparison often generates questions that individual cases cannot.

When readers encounter multiple reports across many years, they can ask:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--comparison" markdown="1">

* Do witness descriptions remain stable over time, or do they change with popular culture?
* Are reports geographically clustered or widely dispersed?
* Which recurring features survive independent reporting?
* Which details appear only after heavy media attention?
* How often do apparently mysterious reports later receive conventional explanations?

</div>

These are historical and comparative questions rather than questions of immediate belief. The archive encourages investigation by highlighting similarities and differences instead of demanding a single explanation.

An equally important consequence is the preservation of anomalies that resist neat classification. Some reports migrate between categories over time. A supposed monster may later become an example of folklore transmission. An alleged haunting may develop into a case study in memory or suggestion. Conversely, apparently mundane reports may become historically significant because similar accounts continue to appear decades later. The archive allows these shifts because older material remains available for reinterpretation rather than being discarded once a news cycle ends. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortean_Times" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fortean Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fortean Times</span></span></span>

## The archive as a living comparative record

Unlike a conventional encyclopaedia, the *Fortean Times* archive is not organised around fixed conclusions. New material continually alters the context in which older reports are understood. Editorial collections, back issues and digital archives make it possible to trace how recurring themes evolve over time, revealing changes in reporting practices, investigative standards and public fascination with particular forms of anomaly. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortean_Times" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fortean Times</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fortean Times</span></span></span>

This is why the archive functions as pattern-making machinery rather than simply storage. Its recurring editorial streams transform disconnected [clippings]({{ 'clippings/' | relative_url }}) into comparable sequences without stripping away ambiguity. Readers are encouraged to recognise families of phenomena, recurring narrative structures and investigative problems, while accepting that many individual cases remain unresolved. In that balance between organisation and uncertainty lies one of the defining strengths of Fortean inquiry.
